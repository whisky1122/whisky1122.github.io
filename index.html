👪 kinelora-family-intelligence - Explore Your Family Story Completely Offline

KinElora is a Windows desktop application for bringing family history together in one calm, clear place. It helps you explore an interactive family tree, understand relationships, record health-risk notes, view numerology insights, follow a family timeline, and keep evidence sources connected to the details you enter. It works offline and is available in 14 languages.

🌟 What KinElora Does

KinElora is built for family research that stays personal. Instead of spreading names, dates, stories, and sources across notebooks and folders, you can organize them in one desktop app on your Windows computer. The app is designed around an offline-first experience, so your family information can remain on your own machine while you work.

The interactive family tree is the center of the experience. You can add family members, connect parents and children, and move through branches to see how people relate to one another. From there, KinElora adds extra views that help you look at family information from different angles: a relationship view, a health-risk section, numerology details, a timeline of events, and evidence sources for the facts you collect.

Once the app is on your Windows computer, open KinElora and choose your preferred language. The app is intended to be easy to approach even if you have never used family-history software before. You can begin with a small branch of your family and add more people, events, and sources as you go.

🧭 First Steps in the App

  1. Choose your language. KinElora supports 14 languages, so you can use the app in the language that feels most comfortable.
  2. Add your first family member. Start with yourself or a relative whose details you already know. You do not need to fill in everything at once.
  3. Build the family tree. Connect people as parents, children, partners, or other relatives so the tree begins to take shape.
  4. Review relationships. Use the relationship views to understand how family members are connected across branches.
  5. Add timeline events. Record births, marriages, moves, milestones, and other family events in date order.
  6. Attach evidence sources. Keep notes about where information came from so your family research stays organized and easier to review later.
  7. Explore extra views. Open the health-risk, numerology, and timeline areas to see your family information presented in different ways.
